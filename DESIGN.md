## Brand & Style
This design system embodies high-end digital craftsmanship, tailor-made for luxury creative web designers, art directors, and digital auteurs. The visual narrative combines the poise of archival print monographs with modern digital minimalism. 

Key attributes:
- **Atmospheric & Tactile**: The canvas evokes heavyweight cotton stock, avoiding clinical pure-white backgrounds in favor of warm, lived-in alabaster and deep umber charcoals.
- **Editorial Poise**: Wide letter-spacing on display typography, generous macro-whitespace, asymmetric layout accents, and hairline delineations replace loud decoration.
- **Understated Confidence**: The user interface serves as a bespoke gallery frame, stepping back to amplify case studies, motion design reels, and curated visual artifacts without competing for attention.

## Colors
The palette relies on subtle tonal contrasts, anchored by an organic warm terracotta tone that commands decisive action without harshness.

- **Primary (`#C88A78`)**: Warm terracotta rose. Applied strictly to primary actions, interactive hover states, active indicators, and high-impact typographic focal points.
- **Background (`#FDFBF7`)**: Warm cream canvas. Provides an organic, non-glare editorial base that softens contrast across the entire viewport.
- **Surface (`#FFFFFF`)**: Pure white reserved for modular cards, raised surfaces, and project presentation modules.
- **Text Primary (`#2B2A29`)**: Deep charcoal with warm undertones. Delivers crisp legibility without the jarring starkness of pure pitch black.
- **Text Secondary (`#7A7572`)**: Muted mineral gray for metadata, captions, timestamps, and secondary navigation labels.
- **Border / Hairline (`#EAE3DE`)**: Subtle warm parchment border used for framing, hairline dividers, and delicate boundary lines.

## Typography
Typographic rhythm sets the intellectual and sensory tone of the design system:

- **Display & Headings (`Playfair Display`)**: Emulates traditional hot-metal letterpress and architectural sign painting. Section titles and case study names are styled uppercase with tracked-out spacing (`0.04em`–`0.12em`) to cultivate stillness and authority.
- **Body & Functional Text (`Plus Jakarta Sans`)**: Neutral, open-aperture grotesque sans that maintains razor-sharp clarity across screen resolutions. Body text maintains a relaxed line-height of `1.6`–`1.7` for effortless reading across multi-paragraph project narratives.
- **Editorial Sub-tier (`title-editorial` & `label-caps`)**: Always set in uppercase with elevated letter spacing for category stamps, client names, and project deliverables.
- **Accents & Marginalia**: Where bespoke callouts appear (such as personalized studio affirmations or signature notes), utilize italicized serif glyphs or hand-rendered script treatments set at lighter weights with relaxed margins.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout honors a generous 12-column fluid grid system pinned to a maximum content width of 1440px to maintain pristine editorial sightlines.

- **Desktop (1024px+)**: 12 columns with `1.5rem` (24px) gutters and `4rem` (64px) canvas side margins. Case study showcases utilize offset column spans (e.g., 7 columns for imagery, 4 columns for accompanying design rationale) to build intentional asymmetry.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px)**: 8 columns with `1.5rem` gutters and `2rem` outer margins. Modules gracefully collapse into stacked pairings.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4 columns with `1rem` gutters and `1.25rem` outer margins. Content simplifies into a monolithic, uninterrupted vertical stream.
- **Macro Rhythm**: Section transitions leverage macro-spacing tokens (`space-2xl` and `space-3xl`) to establish breathing room between distinct creative portfolios and longform client testimonials.

## Elevation & Depth
This design system rejects heavy blurs, artificial drop shadows, and saturated gradients in favor of an architectural, print-inspired depth strategy:

- **Low-Contrast Hairlines**: Structural depth is achieved strictly through crisp 1px borders rendered in `#EAE3DE`. Panels sit directly on `#FDFBF7` or step forward through clean `#FFFFFF` ground.
- **Tonal Stepping**: 
  - Canvas: Base layer (`#FDFBF7`).
  - Active / Resting Containers: Level 1 surface (`#FFFFFF`).
  - Flyout / Navigation Drawer: Elevated surface (`#FFFFFF`) with a micro-stroke hairline and a faint, ambient occlusion shadow: `0 8px 30px rgba(43, 42, 41, 0.04)`.
- **Image Hover Planes**: Visual artifacts, project thumbnails, and interactive cards lift subtly without translation shadows—hover interactions are signaled via slow zoom transitions (scale 1.02), border tone deepening (`#C88A78`), or tonal scrim fades.

## Shapes
A dual-radius geometry governs this design system, balancing structured architecture with tactile warmth:

- **Content & Showcase Containers**: Fixed at `12px` (equivalent to `rounded-lg`). All portfolio cards, media wrappers, mockups, modals, and field groups use this curated contour to soften rectangular grid structures without feeling overly cartoonish.
- **Action & Identity Tokens**: Strict pill-shaped rounding (`9999px`) applies universally to interactive elements: primary/secondary CTA buttons, taxonomy tags, status pills, and floating navigation pills. This distinct contrast ensures visual components of action are instantly distinguished from visual containers of content.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA**: Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), solid `#C88A78` background with `#FFFFFF` text. Padding: `0.75rem 1.75rem`. Typography: `label-md` or `label-caps`. Hover: gentle darkening to `#B87B6A` with smooth 250ms ease.
- **Secondary / Ghost CTA**: Pill-shaped with a 1px `#EAE3DE` border, transparent background, and `#2B2A29` text. Hover: border shifts to `#C88A78` with a faint `#FDFBF7` surface tint.
- **Tertiary Link**: Pure typographic treatment featuring an uppercase label with an animated hairline underline anchored at `0.1em` below the baseline, expanding outward on hover.

### Badges & Category Chips
- Pill silhouette (`rounded-full`), padded with `0.25rem 0.75rem`.
- Subtle warm background (`#F5EFEB`), hairline border (`#EAE3DE`), text styled in `label-caps` in `#7A7572`.
- Active state transitions the background to `#C88A78` with `#FFFFFF` text.

### Cards & Project Showcases
- Surface: `#FFFFFF` card body with explicit 1px `#EAE3DE` hairline boundary and `12px` corner radius.
- Padding inside cards conforms to `space-lg` (24px).
- Imagery within cards carries a matched inner radius (`8px`–`10px`) or bleeds to card top edges with flush clipping.

### Form Inputs & Textareas
- Background: `#FFFFFF` on `#FDFBF7` canvas, framed by a 1px `#EAE3DE` perimeter, rounded at `8px` or `12px`.
- Padding: `0.875rem 1rem`. Typography: `body-md` in `#2B2A29`.
- Placeholder text in `#7A7572`.
- Focus state: Border transitions to `#C88A78` with no aggressive ring or glow, maintaining serene precision.

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- Checkboxes: `18px x 18px` with `4px` corner radius, hairline border `#EAE3DE`. Active fill: `#C88A78` with a pure white minimalist tick.
- Radios: `18px x 18px` circular frame. Active selection displays an inner `#C88A78` solid disc surrounded by an uncolored 2px buffer.

### Editorial Indexes & Lists
- Used for project archives, service menus, and awards tables.
- Monolithic full-width rows divided by 1px horizontal `#EAE3DE` borders.
- Hovering a list item reveals a contextual project image preview or activates an accent state across the row, shifting text from `#2B2A29` to `#C88A78`.
